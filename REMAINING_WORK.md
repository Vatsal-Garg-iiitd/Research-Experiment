# Remaining work

Only items still incomplete relative to the requested study are listed here.

1. **Conventional model scope and budgets.** Mean Teacher, FixMatch and MixMatch now have five labeled fractions (1%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 40%), three seeds and three epochs. They remain compact MLP-based recipes with photometric augmentations, and three epochs cannot establish saturation or reproduce canonical benchmark settings. Other expanded schedules cover MLP, LeNet, ResNet, compact transformer, Random Forest, K-Means, GMM, pseudo-labeling, AE/VAE, DBSCAN sensitivity plus data-size scaling, five RL algorithms, and actual sklearn LabelPropagation (iterations through 2,000, three seeds; convergence reached at tolerance 1e-3). Neural epoch sweeps stop at 5 rather than 10–30; RL stops at 25K rather than 50K/100K. Training uses a fixed 10k subset, not the full Fashion-MNIST training partition. The transformer remains a compact proxy, not standard ViT-Tiny.
2. **Additional supervised metrics and diagnostics.** Some test endpoints include accuracy, macro precision/recall/F1 and confusion matrices, but these are not consistently present for every model. Complete per-budget training/validation loss/accuracy, inference timing, peak device memory and defensible FLOPs are not available for all models.
3. **LLM scope.** Six configurations have actual 50K-token WikiText-2 test evaluation; that does not cover all 15 model configurations. Larger configurations and GPT-2 Medium lack complete long evaluation. Pythia checkpoint trajectories are not 8–12 evenly sampled checkpoints across every feasible model. No original pretraining was run. lm-evaluation-harness smoke subsets completed for three models (Pythia-70M, GPT-2 Small, OPT-125M), 100 examples per task; they are not full official benchmark splits. Full HellaSwag/PIQA/ARC-Easy evaluation remains outstanding. The 50K WikiText evaluations use context 1024 for GPT-2 and 2048 for the others.
4. **NPU.** The AMD XDNA2 NPU is visible to Windows, but the installed project environment has no verified Ryzen AI/VitisAI/Windows ML NPU execution provider. No NPU inference benchmark was completed.
5. **Hardware qualification.** CPU vs DirectML timing exists for one small MLP. Adam emitted an operator CPU-fallback warning; per-process GPU utilization and VRAM could not be isolated. It is not a representative backend-wide comparison. Installed graphics driver at inspection did not meet the cited ROCm package requirement, so no ROCm run is recorded.
6. **Literature extraction.** There are 15 source-attributed configurations, but field-by-field primary-source verification, exact page/table/figure locators, and consistently extracted hardware, duration, epochs, train/validation loss and compatible performance metrics remain incomplete. Blank fields are not missing-data imputations. No broad cross-paper regression or causal scaling claim is justified yet.
7. **Analysis completeness.** Current statistical summaries and marginal increments cover only the expanded sweeps with sufficient data, not every 20-model entry or every requested metric. Three seeds are not available at all budget points for all methods; intervals are conditional on a fixed data split. Short curves are insufficient to claim general saturation/diminishing returns. Additional plots requested for complete model-size scaling, error bars, loss curves, and full hardware comparison depend on the missing measurements.
8. **Operational validation.** The canonical dataset builder, analysis generators and output validator have been run successfully on the current artifacts (430 canonical rows; 458 grouped summaries; 279 marginal increments; zero CSV validation errors or warnings). The validator does not yet verify every report claim against source rows or literature locators, so independent claim-level audit remains needed.
