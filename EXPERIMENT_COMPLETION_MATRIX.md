# Final Completion Matrix

Updated 2026-11-09 from raw-result coverage checks, full-test LLM endpoints, and field-level literature evidence. Conventional status requires all registered budgets, seeds, and final evaluations verified by `scripts/audit_conventional_completion.py`. Literature status requires the source, core scale/token evidence, and every registered field to be classified and located in `scripts/audit_literature_completion.py`; it denotes a completed literature objective and never local reproduction. Double DQN retains its source-mapping correction in derived records only; raw CSV rows are preserved. For the 15 LLM configuration objectives, a completed full WikiText-2 raw-v1 test endpoint counts as a completed configuration. Groupwise downstream-benchmark and checkpoint-trajectory subtasks remain separately disclosed; endpoint evaluation is not pretraining.

**Per-configuration objective completion: 46/50 configurations (92%).** The denominator remains 20 conventional models + 15 specified LLM configurations + 15 literature configurations. The denominator and objective count are unchanged. LLM configuration objectives are counted complete when the full WikiText-2 test endpoint exists for that configuration; the remaining four are interrupted or resource-limited. This count does not imply complete groupwise benchmark or checkpoint-trajectory coverage.
